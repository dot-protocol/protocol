  EXPERIMENT 23: CONCURRENT CHAIN BUILDING
======================================================================

  Observers:  5
  DOTs each:  20  (100 total per phase)

  PHASE 1 — WITHOUT LOCK:
    Total time:           7.9 ms
    DOTs appended:        100
    Threading errors:     0
    Chain link errors:    95  ← race conditions corrupt links
    Chain integrity:      CORRUPTED (expected)

  PHASE 2 — WITH LOCK:
    Total time:           36.2 ms
    DOTs appended:        100
    All sigs valid:       True
    Chain links valid:    True
    Chain integrity:      INTACT ✓

  Lock overhead:  36.2 ms vs 7.9 ms  (+360%)

  KEY INSIGHT:
    A shared chain requires serialized access (lock or single writer).
    Without a lock, race conditions break parent-hash links.
    The DOT protocol is append-only by design — concurrent reads are safe,
    but concurrent writes to a shared chain require coordination.
